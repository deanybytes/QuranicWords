In the Qur'an: Al-Hijr 15:48

لَا يَمَسُّهُمۡ فِيهَا نَصَبٞ وَمَا هُم مِّنۡهَا بِمُخۡرَجِينَ

English

No fatigue will touch them therein, nor from it will they [ever] be removed.
