In the Qur'an: Maryam 19:43

يَٰٓأَبَتِ إِنِّي قَدۡ جَآءَنِي مِنَ ٱلۡعِلۡمِ مَا لَمۡ يَأۡتِكَ فَٱتَّبِعۡنِيٓ أَهۡدِكَ صِرَٰطٗا سَوِيّٗا

English

O my father, indeed there has come to me of knowledge that which has not come to you, so follow me; I will guide you to an even path.
