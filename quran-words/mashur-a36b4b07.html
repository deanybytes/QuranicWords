In the Qur'an: Al-Hijr 15:15

لَقَالُوٓاْ إِنَّمَا سُكِّرَتۡ أَبۡصَٰرُنَا بَلۡ نَحۡنُ قَوۡمٞ مَّسۡحُورُونَ

English

They would say, "Our eyes have only been dazzled. Rather, we are a people affected by magic."
