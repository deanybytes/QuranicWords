In the Qur'an: Al-Baqarah 2:179

وَلَكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡقِصَاصِ حَيَوٰةٞ يَٰٓأُوْلِي ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَتَّقُونَ

English

And there is for you in legal retribution [saving of] life, O you [people] of understanding, that you may become righteous.
