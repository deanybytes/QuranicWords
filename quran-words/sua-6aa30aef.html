In the Qur'an: Ar-Rum 30:10

ثُمَّ كَانَ عَٰقِبَةَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَسَٰٓـُٔواْ ٱلسُّوٓأَىٰٓ أَن كَذَّبُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ وَكَانُواْ بِهَا يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ

English

Then the end of those who did evil was the worst [consequence] because they denied the signs of Allah and used to ridicule them.

Bahasa Indonesia

Puis, mauvaise fut la fin de ceux qui faisaient le mal, ayant traité de mensonges les versets d'Allah et les ayant raillés.
