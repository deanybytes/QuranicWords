In the Qur'an: Qaf 50:19

وَجَآءَتۡ سَكۡرَةُ ٱلۡمَوۡتِ بِٱلۡحَقِّۖ ذَٰلِكَ مَا كُنتَ مِنۡهُ تَحِيدُ

English

And the intoxication of death will bring the truth; that is what you were trying to avoid.
