In the Qur'an: At-Talaq 65:9

فَذَاقَتۡ وَبَالَ أَمۡرِهَا وَكَانَ عَٰقِبَةُ أَمۡرِهَا خُسۡرًا

English

And it tasted the bad consequence of its affair, and the outcome of its affair was loss.
