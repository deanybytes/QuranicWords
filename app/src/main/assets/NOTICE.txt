--------------------------------------------------------------------------
A note on word-pronunciation audio

Word-pronunciation clips (`app/src/main/assets/audio/words/`) are not
sourced from a licensed recitation corpus - they are machine-synthesized
text-to-speech audio, generated per word via the Google Cloud Text-to-Speech
API (WaveNet voice `ar-XA-Wavenet-B`, male). They are a pronunciation aid,
not a recitation/tajweed reference, and are bundled directly in the app
(no runtime download, no API key shipped in the app - synthesis happens
once at content-authoring time). See
`tools/ingestion/12_generate_word_audio.py` and docs/CONTENT_SOURCES.md.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
A note on AI-drafted content

Bangla word-level meanings (`meaning["bn"]`) were originally AI-drafted,
grounded in the sourced English meanings above but not independently
verified against a published Bangla source (no source among those above
provides word-level Bangla glosses, only full-verse translations). 1,526 of
3,680 (41.5%) have since been independently verified against quran.gtaf.org's
own Bangla word-by-word data and replaced where they disagreed; the rest
remain AI-drafted and are tracked as such in the app's own data
(`meaningReviewed["bn"]`) rather than presented as verified. Released under
CC BY-SA 4.0 alongside the sourced Bangla translation content. See
docs/CONTENT_SOURCES.md for the full methodology.
