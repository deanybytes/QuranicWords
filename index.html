❓ Frequently Asked Questions about Quranic Words & Vocabulary

What is QuranicWords and how does it work?
QuranicWords is an open-source educational platform and interactive dictionary designed to teach Quranic Arabic vocabulary. Rather than memorizing random lists, it organizes 4,709 verified Quranic lemmas in descending order of their real occurrence frequency across 10 chapters. By learning the most frequent words first, learners quickly gain comprehension of over 80% of the Qur'anic text.
What languages are supported for Quran word translations?
QuranicWords features verified word-by-word meanings across 11 languages:
  • English (EN)
  • Bengali (বাংলা - BN)
  • Urdu (اردو - UR)
  • Hindi (हिन्दी - HI)
  • Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia - ID)
  • Malay (Bahasa Melayu - MS)
  • Turkish (Türkçe - TR)
  • Persian / Farsi (فارسی - FA)
  • Hausa (HA)
  • Swahili (Kiswahili - SW)
  • French (Français - FR)
How many unique words and root words are in the Qur'an?
The Noble Qur'an contains approximately 77,430 total word tokens. The QuranicWords curriculum indexes 4,709 unique vocabulary lemmas derived from 251 primary Arabic root words, categorized into Particles (173), Verbs (1,479), and Nouns (3,057).
What is Wujūh al-Qur'an (Quranic Polysemy)?
Wujūh al-Qur'an is the classical Islamic science of Quranic polysemy, where an Arabic word holds multiple distinct meanings in different verses and contexts. QuranicWords provides interactive polysemy tabs for all relevant lemmas, showing the precise contextual sense alongside authentic Ayah citations.
Is QuranicWords completely free, offline, and private?
Yes. QuranicWords is 100% free, open source, and ad-free. It requires no login, no accounts, and no data tracking. The web application functions as a Progressive Web App (PWA) that works offline, and a native Android app built with Jetpack Compose is also available on GitHub.
